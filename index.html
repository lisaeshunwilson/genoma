The Bottleneck

We have the tools. We're flying blind.

46 approved gene therapies exist today, covering less than 1% of the 10,000+ known genetic disorders. There's no shared roadmap, no agreed benchmarks, no real way to compare the tools we already have. The bottleneck isn't the absence of gene editing technology. It's the absence of a framework to evaluate it. That's the gap Genoma exists to close.

Three Pillars. One Mission.

How we close the gap.

01

CALI Competition

Critical Assessment of Large Cargo Insertion

A biennial, blind, unbiased benchmarking competition that lets academia and industry compete, compare, and learn from the same open standard.

02

Research Lab

Mitochondrial Gene Therapy

A focused nonprofit lab developing gene therapies for mitochondrial DNA diseases, tackling age-related and rare disorders no one else is chasing.

03

State of the Genome

Public Science Communication

A newsletter, podcast, and forum creating a trusted, structured space for public discourse on gene editing, AI in biology, and what it means for humanity.

Also open-source: BaseRead. Our variant annotation tool turns raw sequencing data into grounded, database-backed variant insight, built in the open alongside everything else we do.

About Genoma

Built by people who've lived the research gap.

Genoma Institute is a small, Berkeley-based nonprofit founded by scientists who moved between structural biology, gene therapy, and AI-driven protein design, and kept hitting the same wall: no shared way to evaluate the tools already in hand.

We're building that evaluation layer in the open, with a growing team of researchers, engineers, and communicators who believe this problem is worth solving in public.
