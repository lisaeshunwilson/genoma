Open Roles

Undergraduate Fellowships: The Read Suite

The Read suite is a family of open-source tools that turn complex biological data into clear, plain-language insight. Each tool is built by an undergraduate fellow working directly with our founder, Dr. Lisa Eshun-Wilson.

01 Now recruiting · Summer 2027

BaseRead Fellow

BaseRead (baseread.vercel.app) turns genetic variant files into plain-language reports by cross-referencing ClinVar, gnomAD, dbSNP, and OMIM. You'll build new features, test the tool with real-world data from our research and hospital partners, and contribute to a preprint and conference presentations.

Looking for: programming experience (Python or JavaScript), interest in genetics or bioinformatics.

Apply →
02 Now recruiting · Summer 2027

EnsembleRead Fellow

Proteins are not frozen shapes. They move between many conformations, and those motions often explain how they work. EnsembleRead will compare structural models, such as AlphaFold predictions, against experimental cryo-EM data to show which conformations fit, and turn that conformational landscape into a clear readout anyone can interpret. You'll help build the tool, explore what different ensembles mean biologically, and contribute to a preprint and presentations.

Looking for: programming experience (Python), interest in structural biology, biochemistry, or biophysics.
