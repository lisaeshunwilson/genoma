Why Intern Here

What you'll actually get.

Real, mission-driven work

Not busywork. Your work directly moves the CALI competition, the research lab, or State of the Genome forward.

Close mentorship

Small team means direct access to the people making the decisions, not buried a layer of management deep.

Hands-on with cutting-edge tools

Cryo-EM, AI-driven protein design, gene therapy research, the kind of exposure most internships don't offer.

Ground-floor ownership

Genoma is early. What you build now shapes how the organization works for years to come.
